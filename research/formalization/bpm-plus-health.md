# BPM+ Health

## Claim

BPM+ Health represents the generic-BPM-standards route to clinical workflow
formalization (BPMN/DMN/CMMN reuse via an OMG-to-HL7 transition), an alternative
strategy to bespoke CIG formalisms like GLIF/Asbru/PROforma or FHIR-native approaches
like CQL/CDS Hooks.

## Evidence

**[measured]** The BPM+ Health / HL7 BPM Community of Practice describes an
OMG-to-HL7 transition and a generic BPMN/DMN/CMMN reuse strategy for healthcare
workflow modeling (2024–2025) ([bpm-plus.org](https://www.bpm-plus.org/)).

**[inference]** This is a third strategic lineage distinct from both horismos's
approach (one ontology owning domain+rules+workflow) and the CQL/CDS-Hooks/FHIR
approach (thin layers on vendor CIS): reuse of general-purpose BPM tooling rather than
a clinical-native formalism. No adoption-outcome evidence for BPM+ Health specifically
was found in this sweep; it is filed here as a landscape data point, not yet assessed
against horismos.

## Source

- BPM+ Health / HL7 BPM Community of Practice — https://www.bpm-plus.org/

## Confidence

measured (existence and framing of the standard); no outcome evidence yet, so no
comparative claim is made.
