# PROforma / Deontics

## Claim

PROforma is a TNM formalism whose own evaluation documented significant
authoring-burden limitations even at narrow (6-pharmacy) scope. Its commercial
descendant, Deontics, is still active, with a vendor-reported (not independently
verified) 33% triage-time figure from an NHS deployment.

## Evidence

**[measured]** "Evaluation of PROforma as a language for implementing medical
guidelines" (2006) documents a 6-pharmacy hypertension trial and authoring-burden
limitations ([PMC1459125](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1459125/)).

**[measured]** Deontics markets itself as PROforma's commercial descendant
([deontics.com/about](https://deontics.com/about/)).

**[measured]** A 2023 press piece reports a Deontics/Guy's & St Thomas' prostate
pathway deployment with a vendor-reported 33% triage-time reduction
([hospitalhealthcare.com](https://hospitalhealthcare.com/news/pioneering-nhs-trust-using-ai-to-help-doctors-make-right-treatment-decisions/)) —
note this figure is vendor-reported, not from an independent peer-reviewed study.
