# Task-Network Model family: Asbru / EON / SAGE / GLARE

## Claim

Asbru, EON, SAGE, and GLARE — grouped here as sharing the same Task-Network Model
lineage as GLIF and PROforma — collectively demonstrate that no TNM formalism reached
sustained, production, multi-site use, despite substantial institutional investment in
each.

## Evidence

**[measured]** A 2018 review of computer-interpretable guidelines for multimorbidity
management confirms the TNM family classification and finds most cited works are
proof-of-concept, not operational
([PMC6172935](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6172935/)).

**[measured]** EON's clinical instantiation, ATHENA-HTN, was a real multi-site VA RCT.
The trial found no significant difference in blood-pressure control from decision
support alone versus a reminder-only control arm
([PMC2243943](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2243943/); trial registry:
https://classic.clinicaltrials.gov/ct2/show/results/NCT00374452). A companion
clinician-feedback analysis is at
[PMC1839712](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC1839712/).

**[measured]** SAGE was a 5-year, NIST-funded, six-institution consortium explicitly
built to solve what GLIF and Arden couldn't (vendor-CIS integration + standard
terminology). No evidence of continuation was found after funding ended around 2006 —
it appears to have quietly stopped rather than been documented as a failure
([clinfowiki.org](https://www.clinfowiki.org/wiki/index.php/The_SAGE_guideline_model)).

**[measured]** GLARE, a 7-year Turin academic collaboration, is self-described by its
own authors as a "domain-independent prototypical system" — research language, not
production language
([Terenziani et al.](https://people.unipmn.it/alessio/doc/terenziani_CPG.pdf)).

**[measured]** The LASSIE versioning case study found only 133 of 515 sentences in one
guideline update were inheritable unchanged — the rest needed re-formalization
([PMC2859225](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2859225/)).

**[measured]** Formal (model-checking) verification of Asbru guidelines with a
purpose-built temporal logic stayed in small academic case studies and never reached
production tooling
([spinroot.com](https://spinroot.com/spin/symposia/ws06/027.pdf)). MobiGuide's own
evaluation was a small-scale patient trial (10 AF + 19 GDM patients)
([springer.com](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11257-017-9190-5)).
