# RxNorm

## Claim

RxNorm's core RXCUI/names are US public domain, but full releases require a free UMLS
license — relevant to a future medication-related horismos scenario (e.g. VTE
prophylaxis's enoxaparin ordering).

## Evidence

**[measured]** NLM's RxNorm Terms of Service: core RXCUI/names are US public domain;
full releases need a free UMLS license
([nlm.nih.gov](https://www.nlm.nih.gov/research/umls/rxnorm/docs/termsofservice.html)).
NLM's RxNav Data Sources page documents the RxTerms/MED-RT ecosystem relationships
([lhncbc.nlm.nih.gov](https://lhncbc.nlm.nih.gov/RxNav/information/DataSources.html)).
